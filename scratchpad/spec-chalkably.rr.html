In scope

Seven existing controls, vanilla and React, deterministic seeds, chalk states, reduced motion, demo, docs, tests, and MIT attribution.

Not in scope

New controls, canvas, new frameworks, a hosted docs site, npm publication, a light-surface theme, or a general design system.

 The quality bar is recognition at a glance without losing focus, forms, labels, or readable motion.

The DOM layer owns lifecycle. The pure renderer owns seeded paths. CSS owns chalk palette, layer density, focus, state, and motion.
